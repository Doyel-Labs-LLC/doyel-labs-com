# BAI Risk Disclosure

Read this before you arm BAI for the first time, and again whenever it changes.

1. **You can lose money, including all of it.** Stocks fall, gaps skip your stop, and a
   "protective" order is a request your broker fills at whatever price the market gives it,
   not a guarantee.

2. **Automated software fails in ways a person would not.** It can act on a stale or wrong
   quote, send an order twice, miss an exit, or stop entirely when your computer sleeps, loses
   its network, or updates. BAI has safeguards for each of these. None of them is perfect.

3. **Armed means unattended.** When you arm the desk, it enters trades without asking you,
   inside the caps you typed. If those caps are wrong, the trades will be wrong. Check them.
   Keep the kill switch reachable.

4. **Backtests, replays and paper results are not predictions.** They are the past, seen
   through rules chosen after the fact. Real fills are worse than simulated ones.

5. **Nobody at Doyel Labs LLC is licensed to advise you,** and the app's outputs are not
   advice. If you need advice, speak to a licensed professional.

6. **Your broker is the record.** If the app and your broker disagree, your broker is right.
   Log in to your broker directly whenever you want certainty.

7. **Pattern day trading rules and margin** can restrict or amplify what happens in your
   account. Know your broker's rules before you arm.
